# Why ChemAgent

- **Pinned inputs** connect datasets and saved parsing profiles to an analysis.
- **Recorded rounds** expose proposals, executed parameters, scoped metrics and diagnostics.
- **Managed models** connect reports, source datasets and prediction workflows.
- **Analytical references** support decisions through the method library and knowledge sources.

Available capabilities depend on deployment configuration, tools and input data. Missing historical diagnostic points are shown explicitly; viewing a page does not reconstruct them.
